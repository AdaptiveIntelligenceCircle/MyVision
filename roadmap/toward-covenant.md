# Toward Covenant

| Stage | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Pre-Covenant** | Specs, prototypes, parallel tests, letters — **current** |
| **Covenant** | Declared main governance surface: full Charter discipline, published residual risks |
| **Post-founder emphasis** | Succession drills, multi-admin, reduced single-point narrative |

Covenant is declared when process and refusal quality deserve the word — not when a calendar or a meeting feels exciting.

**Target horizon for early public phase testing** has been discussed in the long range (e.g. late 2027). Treat as intent.
