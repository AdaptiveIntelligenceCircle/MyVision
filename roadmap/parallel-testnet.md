# Parallel TestNet orientation

## Idea

Run **multiple reference environments** under one principle family:

- shared Ethical Kernel constraints,  
- different surfaces (lab / public test / domain pilots),  
- controlled bridges later — not silent merge of rules.

## Why

- Diversify failure modes.  
- Experiment without rewriting history of constraints.  
- Avoid “one mainnet hype event = one political target.”

## What it is not

- Not a claim of production global WMI.  
- Not an excuse for infinite unfinished forks.  
- Not token liquidity design.

Community may be thin while parallel work is real. **Under-claim.**
