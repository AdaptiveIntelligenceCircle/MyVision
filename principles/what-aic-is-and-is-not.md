# What AIC is / is not

## AIC is

- An **independent**, open-source-oriented project to build an **ethical coordination layer** for human–AI systems.  
- A stack of constraints: **Ethical Kernel**, human meaning gates, time-bounded roles, auditability.  
- Aimed at long-term **World Meaning Infrastructure (WMI)** — substrate, not a single consumer app.  
- **Third Path**: refuses pure zero-sum state capture *and* pure commercial rule-power over the protocol.  
- Founder-led in early phases, with an explicit goal to **reduce single-person dependency** over time.

## AIC is not

- A **United Nations** body, program, agency, or endorsement.  
- An **arm, vendor ethics skin, or subsidiary narrative** for Big Tech.  
- A **token-voting** scheme where capital buys protocol law.  
- A political party instrument or a replacement for **national law**.  
- A finished global network or a guarantee of civilizational outcomes.

## Short English blurb (meetings)

> AIC builds an independent ethical coordination layer — principles in the kernel, human meaning gates, time-bounded power. Not UN, not Big Tech, not token rule-power.
