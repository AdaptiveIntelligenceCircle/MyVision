# Entity ≠ immunity

A legal entity, fiscal host, or **strategic partnership** may help with contracting, accounting, and legitimacy signaling.

It does **not**:

- erase **Vietnamese law**, **EU law**, or other applicable law;  
- cancel contracts, data protection duties, sanctions, export controls, or tax;  
- override AIC’s **Charter** or **Ethical Kernel**;  
- mean a partner **owns** the protocol or may sell rule-power.

**Partner ≠ owner ≠ regulator ≠ shield.**

We state this so public language cannot be spun into false cover or false capture.
